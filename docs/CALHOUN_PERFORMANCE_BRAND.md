# CALHOUN PERFORMANCE — the brand

**This is Cole's brand, supplied by Cole. It is GIVEN, not a design deliverable.**
No council round, agent, or engine redesigns it. Aurelius's job is to *operationalize*
it — speak in it, make content and outreach in it, generate demand for it. Where the
verbal brand below is marked DRAFT, it is Aurelius's inference from the visual identity
plus Cole's business context, and Cole overwrites it; the visual identity is locked.

---

## Name

**Calhoun Performance.** ("Calhoun" is the wordmark; "Performance" is the descriptor tag.)

## Visual identity — LOCKED (read off the supplied logos)

- **Palette: strict monochrome.** Black, white, and light grey only. No color, by choice.
  Reads premium, serious, disciplined. Any Aurelius-generated visual/landing asset holds
  this — high-contrast black/white, never a color accent.
- **The mark:** a stylized muscular athlete figure — powerful build, tousled/curly hair,
  caught mid-motion (arms raised, a throw/press) — set inside a forward-slanted blade /
  parallelogram shield. Classical-strength energy (a modern Hercules / discus-thrower)
  rendered as a contemporary athletic emblem. The icon works standalone (avatar, watermark).
- **Wordmark typography:** a custom **condensed, forward-slanted (italic), aggressive**
  athletic sans — motorsport / performance feel. "PERFORMANCE" sits smaller and bold.
- **Overall feeling:** power, motion, discipline, classical-meets-modern. Aspirational and
  hard, not hype-y or soft. Serious about strength.

## Brand personality (from the visual) — the voice constraints

- Direct. No fluff, no hype, no emoji-spam, no "hope this finds you well."
- High standard. Aspirational but earned — it looks like a brand that expects work.
- Disciplined and premium. Monochrome restraint is the tell: it doesn't shout in color, it
  carries weight in contrast. Copy should have the same restraint — say less, mean it.
- Motion/power in the language: strong verbs, forward energy, nothing passive.

---

## Verbal brand — CONFIRMED BY COLE

- **The mantra / brand line: "Move fast, lift heavy, be an athlete."** This is the through-
  line for all copy, content, and outreach. It matches the mark (motion + power + the athlete
  figure) and it IS the positioning: strength AND speed AND athleticism, not one without the
  others. Every hook, every angle, every asset should ladder back to it.
- **Who it's for (confirmed):** **high school and college athletes** (primary), with **some
  general population** as a secondary. Parents typically buy for the school-age athletes.
- **The gen-pop wedge (from the mantra):** the pitch to a general-pop client is
  **"train like an athlete, not a gym-goer"** — "be an athlete" is the aspiration that lets a
  non-competitive adult buy the same standard. This is a real positioning edge, not an
  afterthought; gen pop who want to move and feel athletic, not just lift.
- **The edge / method (from CLAUDE.md, high-confidence):** athletes who *understand and can
  leverage their own bodies* — not compliance-followers. Cole is a **standard-setter, not a
  guarantee coach**: he states what's required and why, never "we'll get you X." The tracked
  numbers are the proof.
- **Sold as (from profile):** monthly retainer · 8–12 week block · one-off program.

*Still Cole-supplied when he's ready: the exact promise wording, the longer vision, a voice
sample (a caption/post he's proud of) for the compiled layer to learn his cadence, and the
offer's price.*
