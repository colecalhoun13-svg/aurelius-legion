# THE SUPER-COACH — the best assistant coach that ever existed, as software, inside observations-only

## The end-state, in his gym and his remote practice

It's a Tuesday testing day. Cole films Marcus's 10-yard on his phone; the clip lands on the Mini. Before Cole has walked back to the whiteboard, the record wall has a new line — *Marcus, 1.58, third-fastest on the roster ever, first sub-1.60 of his junior year* — and a signal waits: *"Hips opened a frame earlier than his May rep; first two steps covered more ground. The 10 dropped where his broad jump climbed — the same posterior chain showing up twice."* Not "fix his arm action." Not "add three sets." An **observation**, sourced to two clips and one number, handed to a coach who will make the call. That is the whole product: an assistant coach with a photographic memory of every rep every athlete has ever done, an eye that never blinks, and the discipline to never once open its mouth about what to *do*.

In the remote practice it's the same organ pointed at a webcam and a shared sheet — client #1 films a trap-bar triple in a garage in another state, and Cole gets the bar-path read and the PR callback the same evening he would have gotten it standing next to them.

The constitutional line runs under all of it: **Aurelius sees, remembers, and notices. Cole decides.** Everything below is greatness *inside* that line.

## The five organs, on the foundations that exist

**1 — The Eye (film breakdown at full power).** Today `vision.ts` fails over three providers for stills and does a single Gemini pass on short inline video with an observations-only prompt (`VIDEO_PROMPT`). The Mini lane (DEPLOY_MAC_MINI §9: whisper.cpp + ffmpeg frame extraction + local pose) is the canvas. End-state: every clip becomes a **structured kinematic trace** — bar path as a plotted line with velocity loss per rep, jump mechanics as hip/knee/ankle angle-time curves, sprint as ground-contact and step-length series — computed locally, stored as numbers, not prose. The read is phrased exactly as `trendSweep` already phrases training: *"bar drifted forward 4cm on rep 4 vs rep 1; velocity fell 22%"* — a measured fact, never *"keep it over midfoot."* The line it must never cross: **kinematics describe, they never correct.** The prompt lock in `VIDEO_PROMPT` and the G5 prescription-scanner the council already specified are the guardrails; the Mini pipeline inherits both. An angle is an observation; a cue is a coaching call, and it stays Cole's.

**2 — The Memory (every rep, for years, as one queryable mind).** `performance.ts` already holds each athlete's full series with momentum and relative strength; `battery.ts` ranks the roster. The leap is **time**: normalize every curve by age and grad-year so a sophomore's 30-inch vert is read against *where sophomores go*, not against the roster's seniors. Aurelius becomes the coach who can answer *"how does Marcus's junior-year 10-yard curve compare to every junior I've ever tracked?"* — long-term athletic development curves as a first-class object, the whole roster's history as one recallable corpus (this is why real embeddings + the nightly distiller in the council's Tier 3/4 are load-bearing: without them the memory forgets what Cole said about an athlete two days ago). The line: **the memory reports position and trajectory; it never says whether the trajectory is "good enough"** — that judgment is coaching. It shows Cole the curve; he reads it.

**3 — The Pattern-Sense (what one human can't hold across 30 athletes).** `seriesMomentum` already computes stalled/regressing/streak per series; `trendSweep` fires one deduped signal per athlete Mondays. End-state widens the lens from one athlete to the **roster as a field**: *which stimulus moved which athletes* (the athletes whose vert jumped all logged heavy trap-bar the prior block — a correlation surfaced, never a prescription), *who is trending toward a stall two weeks before `stalled` would trip* (slope decay, not the 28-day threshold), and the **readiness signal before the injury** — a quiet drop in bar velocity at a fixed load, a jump losing height while strength holds, phrased strictly as `kind:"training"`/health signals the way NORTH_STAR already permits Aurelius to speak in training and health domains. The line, and it is the sharpest one here: **a readiness signal names what changed in the numbers ("Jake's bar speed at 225 fell three sessions running"); it never names a diagnosis or a deload.** Signals in training/health, decisions to Cole — hard rule 5, verbatim.

**4 — The Athlete's Experience (the artifact that makes them train).** This organ barely exists — `batteryRecords` builds a record wall, but no athlete has ever *seen* it. Build the **progress artifact**: the athlete's own record wall, their PR reel (the clip that set the mark, saved), their curve against their grad-year cohort — the thing a 16-year-old screenshots and sends his friends, the thing that makes him show up. For minors this is **consent-gated to the parent** (the `isMinor` flag exists; the sharing surface does not — it must be built as a permissioned outward read, and any outward share is Cole's confirm by construction). The line: **the athlete sees records and trajectories, never the coaching read.** The candid observation ("hips rise early, watch fatigue") is Cole's-eyes-only — the reasoner already writes "Cole's eyes only," and the council's Fix-6 flags exactly the danger of that text leaking to a shared sheet. The athlete gets the record; Cole gets the diagnosis.

**5 — The Mirror (Cole's own coaching development).** The apprentice idea, turned on its owner. Aurelius watches *how Cole coaches* — which athletes he films, which stalls he acts on and which he lets ride, how his corrections cluster — and reflects **patterns about his own practice**: *"You've flagged five posterior-chain stalls this month and cued the same three; the two you didn't cue are the two still stalled."* An observation about Cole, offered to Cole. The line is identical to all the others and it is why this is safe: **it observes his coaching, it never tells him how to coach.** It makes him sharper by holding up the mirror, never by grabbing the wheel.

## The ONE that most changes the athletes' actual outcomes

**The readiness/stall-precursor signal (organ 3), riding the local Eye (organ 1).** A record wall is a scoreboard; a memory is an archive; the mirror sharpens the coach slowly. But the signal that says *"this athlete is bending toward a stall — or toward a strain — before it shows on the sheet"* is the one that changes what actually happens to a body. It's the difference between Cole catching a decaying bar-velocity trend in week two and finding out in week six when the number finally drops. It stands on code that exists (`seriesMomentum`, `trendSweep`, the vision lane) and needs only the Mini's velocity trace plus a slope-based early trip — and it lives most naturally inside observations-only, because a precursor is *by nature* a signal, not a prescription: it hands Cole a two-week head start and lets him spend it however his judgment says. That head start, multiplied across thirty athletes a human coach cannot all watch at once, is the super-coach's real gift.
