# THE HOLE-FINDER — Life-coverage audit

**1) Packed day, session runs over.** 3:00pm session bleeds to 3:50; the 4pm athlete, the drive, and the 6pm call all cascade. A second operator watches the clock and re-sequences: "skip the drive-through errand, text the 6pm you're 15 late, your 4pm's prep note still stands." Aurelius today has exactly three fixed touches — 07:00, 13:00, 21:30 (`index.ts` spine). The 13:00 midday check (`planning/tools.ts::runMiddayCheck`) counts *task completion ratio* only — it never reads the calendar, so a schedule in collapse at 3pm with tasks done is invisible. Calendar conflict scanning exists but only for events overlapping *protected focus blocks* (`calendar/engine.ts:449-490`). There are no event-start reminders, no travel-time math, no "next event in 10 min" push. The NOW layer knows countdowns *inside a prompt*, but only if Cole asks. **Fix: small build** (an in-day horizon watcher was already named as frontier in NORTH_STAR — "event-driven prep + due-task firing" — and never built).

**2) Testing day, 12 athletes, spotty wifi.** `training/battery.ts` is genuinely good — one-pass entry, canonical labels, PR detection. But its only invoker is the Athletes web page (`frontend/app/(chrome)/athletes/page.tsx`). The PWA has **no service worker** (`frontend/public/` has icons only) — spotty gym wifi means the page dies mid-entry, and there's no local queue/retry. Telegram capture (`captureSplit.ts`) files `athlete_note` free-text but doesn't route "Jake vert 31.5, broad 9'2" into `logBattery` — numbers land as prose, not metrics, so PRs and the record wall never see them. A second operator takes dictation into the ledger. **Fix: small build** (battery grammar in captureSplit → `logMetric`), plus **config/small build** for offline queueing.

**3) Warm-lead DM mid-coaching.** The DM arrives on Instagram; Aurelius has no DM ingestion (inbound capture = `/intake` form, Gmail reply-matching, Twilio — `index.ts:231`, `crm/sms.ts`). So the lead exists only if Cole remembers to forward it. If he Telegrams "lead: Marcus's dad asked about remote coaching," captureSplit files it with `nextActionAt` = *now* (`leadEngine.ts:96`) — good. But the outreach sweep runs **07:30 next morning**, drafts to *Gmail* — the wrong channel for a DM conversation — and caps at 3/run. The lead's 48h: no same-day nudge, no "reply tonight while it's warm" signal, and the follow-up spine assumes email. A hot inbound lead is treated at the cadence of a cold warm-list name. **Fix: small build** (inbound leads get an immediate high-salience Bridge push + channel-aware draft), **habit** (forward DMs immediately).

**4) Sick for 3 days.** `/quiet 3d sick` (`planning/quiet.ts`) is well built — but *Cole must invoke it while sick*, which is exactly when he won't. Nothing notices he's gone dark: no dead-man's switch (no responses, no completions, no habit taps for 36h → propose quiet). Meanwhile quiet pauses only the four pushy rituals; **habit streaks still break** (nothing marks sick days excused), the debrief's streak sentinel is paused so streaks die silently, tasks go overdue and greet him with a wall of red, and `promises.ts` lapse-sweeps him into "lapsed" on commitments illness ate. Quiet shifts *lead* follow-ups but not task due-dates or promises. A second operator declares the sick day *for* him and triages re-entry. **Fix: small build** (auto-propose quiet on inactivity; quiet shifts tasks/promises and freezes streaks).

**5) Sunday plan → Tuesday divergence.** Weekly planning (Sun 18:00) produces a skeleton; when Tuesday blows it up, nothing *replans the week* — the debrief's tomorrow-watch (`rituals/engine.ts:395-426`) and midday check operate day-at-a-time. No "the week's plan is now fiction — here are the two things to rescue by Friday" until *next* Sunday. The dawn-thread checks one task, not the week's shape. **Fix: small build** (a Wednesday-ish drift check against the Sunday skeleton), partially **habit** (`/plan` exists on Telegram, if he thinks to use it).

**6) First paying client, day 0–14.** The strongest coverage found: `crm/onboarding.ts` lays the 5-task runway, retention sweep (08:30) has cadence from `checkInEveryDays=7`. Holes: the runway is tasks *about* artifacts, not artifacts — day-0 welcome and day-1 intake aren't drafted (inbox triage drafts *replies*, not first-touch messages; the outreach drafter serves *leads*, and this person just stopped being one). Delivery itself is Sheets, deliberately parked (`delivery_scope`) — fine — but if the client's payment is Venmo and Gmail isn't connected (checklist item 9), the first invoice's "paid" is manual and "overdue" fires against a payment Aurelius couldn't see. **Fix: small build** (auto-draft the welcome/intake into the runway tasks), **config** (Gmail + Stripe keys).

**7) His own training + a nagging injury.** The biggest identity gap: CLAUDE.md says Cole "tracks his own lifting," and *nothing in the code holds it*. All of `training/` and `crm/retention.ts` key on `clientId` — Cole isn't a Client, has no sheet slot, no PR ledger, no trend sweep watching *him* (`trendSweep.ts` iterates `prisma.client`). The workaround — enroll himself as `training_only` — is undocumented and semantically wrong. And "injury" appears nowhere in the training path (grep: only intake-question copy). If he captures "elbow's been barking on pulls, third week," it files as a note; nothing accumulates recurring pain mentions into the one signal type the constitution *explicitly permits* (signals in training/health domains). A second operator says "third elbow mention in three weeks — that's a pattern." **Fix: small build** (Cole-as-athlete row + a pain-mention accumulator feeding a health signal).

**8) Phone dead / offline in gym.** Everything pocket-side is Telegram (needs data) or the PWA (no service worker, no offline shell, no queued capture). Phone dead = Aurelius mute *and* deaf; nothing accounts for a silent day (see #4 — same missing inactivity awareness). Voice notes, the natural gym-floor capture, require the Telegram round-trip. **Fix: small build** (SW + offline capture queue is cheap and PWA-native), **config** (nothing helps a dead battery — but re-entry triage would).

## Top 5 by trust erosion

1. **No in-day awareness (scenario 1).** The "second operator" claim dies the first afternoon Aurelius watches a cascade happen at 13:00-silent. It knows the calendar and says nothing until 21:30.
2. **His own body is untracked (7).** Aurelius knows twelve athletes' elbows and not Cole's. For a coach who lifts, that's the system not knowing *him*.
3. **Sick mode requires being well enough to invoke it (4).** Broken streaks + lapsed promises + overdue wall after three bad days reads as punishment, not partnership.
4. **Hot lead handled cold (3).** The one thing the business needs — a lead — gets next-morning email cadence. One lost first-client conversation burns disproportionate trust.
5. **No offline/entry resilience where he actually works (2/8).** Losing a testing day's numbers to gym wifi once is enough to send him back to paper.

Common root: the spine is *cron-shaped* while Cole's life is *event-shaped*. Every hole above is the gap between scheduled touches and reacting to what just happened.
