## 1. The trust ladder today

**What Cole confirms and where it chafes.** Ungranted-inward and all outward asks flow through `executor.ts`. The severity split (outward → critical/push, inward → attention/batched) fixed the worst chafe — publish confirms that could never reach his phone. The flywheel exists (`trustLedger.ts::suggestNextGrant`: ≥3 confirms, 0 undos → suggest, 14-day cooldown) but it is confirm-count-gated, so classes Cole rarely encounters never earn a suggestion.

## 3. Where the machinery could break the constitution — and the guard

**The `knowledge.apply_proposal` finalizer enforces neither of its own constitutional guards.** The invariant "autonomy and persona scopes never auto-apply; only research/ingestion-born proposals ride the keyhole" lives *only in callers*: `proposals.ts:166-167` (`fromIngestion && scope !== ESCALATION_SCOPE && scope !== "persona"`) and `queueSweep.ts:51-59` (`ELIGIBLE`). The finalizer itself (`registerActions.ts:100-121`) calls `resolveProposal(..., decision: "confirmed")` for **any** `proposalId` in its payload, and `resolveProposal` checks only `status === "pending"` — no scope, no origin. Once the grant is live, any third caller of `executeAction({actionClass: "knowledge.apply_proposal", ...})` — a future workflow, a refactor, a mis-built payload — finalizes a chat-born `persona.*` or even `autonomy.*` proposal under the grant, receipts intact, constitution broken silently. Contrast `checkGrantable()`, which does defense-in-depth on the `autonomy.` key prefix itself. **The guard:** re-derive scope and origin from the proposal row *inside the finalizer* and throw for `scope in {autonomy, persona}` or non-research/ingestion origin whenever `updatedBy === "aurelius"` — making the choke point enforce what the callers currently promise. (Secondary hardening, same file: the `autonomy.apply_grant` finalizer stamps `grantedBy: "cole"` unconditionally; it should assert its invocation path is a Bridge/Telegram confirm, since `confirmAction` in `executor.ts:240` runs any finalizer "regardless of grant state" and the frontend confirm route, `frontend/app/api/autonomy/confirm/route.ts`, carries no caller authentication.)

The two-lock grant store is excellent; the keyhole deserves the same paranoia.
