**A1 — The keyhole finalizer enforces none of its own constitutional guards.** `aurelius/autonomy/registerActions.ts:100-121` calls `resolveProposal(..., decision: "confirmed")` for **any** `proposalId` in its payload; `resolveProposal` (`aurelius/knowledge/proposals.ts:267-278`) checks only `status === "pending"` plus the atomic claim — no scope, no origin.
