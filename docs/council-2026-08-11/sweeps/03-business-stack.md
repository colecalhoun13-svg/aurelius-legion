## THE BUSINESS STACK — full-coverage sweep (33 files, all read in full)

### Per-module verdicts

**crm/service.ts** — Client Engine core (Lead→Client→Engagement→Session→Invoice→Payment). **A-.** Money is integer cents converted once (`toCents`, service.ts:36); invoice paid-state is sum-recomputed in a transaction, never incremented (service.ts:491-524); overdue derived at read (service.ts:563); gym boundary enforced at every money write (addEngagement:309, raiseInvoice:438, recordPayment:485) and at read (outstandingInvoices:548, pipelineSnapshot:646-650). Minor: `clientDetail`/`findClientByName` serve both kinds (defensible — coaching surface).

**crm/leadEngine.ts** — the acquisition door. **B.** Two real defects:
- **Unbounded follow-up loop** (leadEngine.ts:235-243 + 264-322): `finalizeOutreachDraft` rotates `nextActionAt` +4d and the sweep re-drafts anything due. There is no per-lead touch cap, so a lead who never replies gets a follow-up draft every 4 days *forever* — touch 9, touch 10 — burning the 3/run cap and LLM spend on dead leads while newer leads queue behind them.
- **Phantom "follow-up" framing** (leadEngine.ts:134-137): `priorTouches` counts BridgeSignals from `executeAction`, which are created even when the draft is *gated and never confirmed*. If `outreach.draft` isn't granted, day 1 files a pending proposal, day 2's sweep sees priorTouches=1 and drafts "This is a FOLLOW-UP. Acknowledge that lightly" to a person who has received nothing. (The lead's nextActionAt only rotates in the finalizer, so an unconfirmed lead is re-picked daily.)
- Clean: no-email leads throw instead of being falsely marked contacted (:216-221); inbound capture is directive-defused, length-capped, dedup-keyed email→phone→name-6h (:420-431); push cap of 5/day (:501).

**crm/selfRecord.ts** — self-recording money. **A.** Stripe HMAC verified timing-safe with replay tolerance (:27-46); idempotency keyed on payment_intent with the DB `@@unique([externalRef])` (schema.prisma:1089) as the real TOCTOU guard, P2002 treated as success (:205-213); email-parsed payments go to confirm, never auto-record (spoofable From, :124); auto-match is verified-identifier-only, kind:"client" only (:232). One gap: **no refund/dispute handling** — `charge.refunded` is ignored, so earned never decrements; a refunded month reads as revenue forever.

**crm/ledger.ts** — **B-.** `moneyLedger` aggregates *all* Payment rows with **no `client: { kind: "client" }` filter** (ledger.ts:40-53), unlike `pipelineSnapshot` which filters (service.ts:649-650). Same in **measurement/scoreboard.ts:81-83** (`earnedThisWeek/AllTime`). Today unreachable-in-practice (recordPayment guards writes), but the repo's own stated policy is "exclusion lives in the queries, not display filters" — a hand-edited or pre-guard row leaks into the ledger, the analyst (`channelPerformance` reads `ledger.byChannel`), and the weekly scoreboard. This is the one gym-boundary query miss in the territory.

**crm/retention.ts / targets.ts / performance.ts / onboarding.ts** — **A.** PR peak routes training-only athletes to the training domain with no referral/proof machinery (retention.ts:87-98); proof content refuses training-only (:270) and anonymises minors by default (:282, 294); draftClientMessage addresses the parent for minors (:339); runway is double-lay-guarded (onboarding.ts:58); performance math is unit-bucketed and tie-broken (council fixes visible and correct). `logMetric`'s PR check loads all priors' values — fine at this scale.

### Top 5 findings by severity
1. **paidBoost: confirm records spend that never happened + stager unreachable** (paidBoost.ts:113-129; no caller of stageBoost) — money-ledger dishonesty plus hard-rule-8 recurrence.
2. **Outreach follow-up has no touch cap and mis-labels gated drafts as follow-ups** (leadEngine.ts:134-137, 235-243) — infinite 4-day re-drafting; "follow-up" copy to never-contacted people when the grant is off.
3. **Gym boundary missed in moneyLedger and scoreboard aggregates** (ledger.ts:40-53, scoreboard.ts:81-83) — the only "exclusion in the query" violations found.
4. **No refund handling in the Stripe path** (selfRecord.ts:48) — earned is monotonically wrong after any refund.
5. **Dead lead-magnet + DM flood gap** — benchmarks.ts and partnership.ts unreachable; instagram/messages.ts uncapped lead/signal creation.

### "50 leads and 5 paying clients next month — what breaks first?"
Not the money path — cents math, idempotency, and invoice reconciliation would hold. **The outreach follow-up spine breaks first**: 50 leads all carry `nextActionAt`, the sweep drafts 3/day (17 days just for first touches), and every touched-but-silent lead re-enters the queue every 4 days forever — within three weeks the 3-slot cap is saturated by repeat follow-ups to non-responders, fresh leads starve, and Cole's Bridge fills with drafts he can't review. Second breakage: 5 paying clients on mixed shapes trigger check-in/renewal/PR signals that all land as individual "attention" Bridge items with no digest — signal volume outruns the review capacity the sweep cap was designed around. Third: the first Venmo refund or partial-month churn makes the ledger silently overstate earned.

### 3 best opportunities
1. **Wire "The Standard"** — a finished, pure, zero-cost lead magnet needing only a route + intake hook (`source: "assessment"` already exists in LEAD_SOURCES waiting for it). Highest leverage per line of code in the repo.
2. **Touch-cap + dormancy policy in the sweep** (e.g., 3 touches then `dormant` with one closing signal) — converts the follow-up engine from a future nag machine into the discipline it intends, and fixes finding 2 with ~10 lines.
3. **Unify the kind-filter into the read layer** — a shared `businessPayments()` where-clause used by ledger, scoreboard, and analyst, so the gym boundary is one definition instead of per-query vigilance; same pattern would retire partnership.ts's stale grounding translation via `groundingFromResearch`.
